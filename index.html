Knowledge Maintenance Agent — Demo

Built by Anahita Jacobsen · Concept demo

The agent watches the official source documents. When one changes, it works out which hub pages are affected, explains the change in plain words, and drafts the update. It never publishes anything. A human decides, every time.

Concept demo. Northfield Manufacturing does not exist. Neither do its people or documents. This page is not connected to any real system, and nothing you click is saved: refresh the page and the demo resets.
